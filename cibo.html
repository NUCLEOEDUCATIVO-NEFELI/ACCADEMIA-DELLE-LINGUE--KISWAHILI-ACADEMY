Chakula na Mgahawa

Food and Restaurant

🇮🇹 In Italiano

La cucina swahili è un mosaico di influenze africane, arabe, indiane e persiane. I piatti base includono ugali (polenta di mais), wali (riso), nyama choma (carne arrostita) e pilau (riso speziato). Il ristorante si chiama mgahawa o hoteli, mentre il bar è baa.

Cultura: Mangiare con la mano destra è tradizionale, lavarsi le mani prima e dopo è essenziale.

🇬🇧 In English

Swahili cuisine is a mosaic of African, Arab, Indian and Persian influences. Staple dishes include ugali (maize porridge), wali (rice), nyama choma (grilled meat) and pilau (spiced rice). A restaurant is called mgahawa or hoteli, while a bar is baa.

Culture: Eating with the right hand is traditional, washing hands before and after is essential.

Dialogo al Ristorante / Restaurant Dialogue

Mhudumu (Cameriere):
Karibu mgahawani! Tafadhali kaa. Unataka kunywa nini?
Welcome to the restaurant! Please sit down. What would you like to drink?
Mteja (Cliente):
Asante. Nataka chai na maji baridi. Menyu iko wapi?
Thank you. I want tea and cold water. Where is the menu?
Mhudumu:
Hapa. Leo tunalo ugali na samaki, wali na kuku, au pilau. Unataka nini?
Here it is. Today we have ugali with fish, rice with chicken, or pilau. What do you want?
Mteja:
Ninataka pilau na mboga. Naomba pilipili kidogo. Bei gani?
I want pilau with vegetables. I'd like a little pepper. How much is it?
Mhudumu:
Shilingi elfu tatu. Chakula kitakuja hivi karibuni.
Three thousand shillings. The food will come very soon.
